- Laine follows Kany back to the Inn. Aksel runs into her but they only exchange a few words as he continues on to the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Melted Temple</span>.  
- Arkan informs <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Shasenaya</span> of their current progress into the investigation, which she is thankful for but also does not know how to proceed. She is preparing a burial for the body that was found nearby. Arkan offers the theory that the cult has something to do with Tofrar, which she denies.  
- Aksel finds Arkan and tells him about the Baron's meeting and they go to find Soren during his studying. They exchange the information about the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Cytillesh</span> and the opportunity to investigate the Baron through Egil's delivery.  
- After buying some nondescript clothes for Soren, to replace his priestly garments during the delivery, we bring the cart up to the mansion. The guards in front are dazed, by a nonmagical effect, so we can easily bullshit our way through. Inside, we encounter <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Ivred</span>, the steward of the mansion who seems very busy and disappears after telling us where to bring the decorations.  
- Aksel distracts them by secretly dropping a deer head from the wall, allowing Laine to sneak further into the mansion. Arkan sneaks into the steward's office and finds an itinerary and seating chart. The only name we recognize is <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/khela-olgard/">Khela Olgard</a>. Her name, with a few others (List) is written in red. There is also the outline of a speech (Quote?) which proves some association by knowledge, though the baron (presumably) does not seem to care if the cult continues, as long as they don't bother him or his people. We add our names to the seating chart next to other red names but away from Khela  
- Laine explores and behind a secret door finds a gnome at a desk who mistakes them for a guard and is terrified. He seems to be taken prisoner there to perform some task. Apart from a bed, there is some golden powder, presumably <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Cytillesh</span>. After Laine reveals they are not with the Baron, the gnome clings to them and begs them to take him with. His name is <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Willy</span> and he has been locked up to work on a "cure", an antidote for Cytillesh.  
- Laine puts him in one of the empty crates and carries him towards Egil's. We finish up and get sent off with the payment. Just when we meet Laine shortly before Egil's, a woman talks to us on the street, asking if we as adventurers could help recover a stolen artpiece. She tries to get us to follow her, but we notice a hidden motive, and the symbol of the cult on a necklace. Finally she sighs and reveals an ambush.  
- We successfully fight off the cultists, killing two and leaving one alive. The guards take our statements and put us under watch for the next day in case any more questions come up.
