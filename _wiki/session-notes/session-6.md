- We meet a short distance away on a roof to discuss. Laine shares their findings about the magic Khela used and the guarded room they saw before being escorted out. We plan to sneak to the back to check the room out secretly.  
	- Aksel manages to look inside and spots a large storage room and four people inside reading documents and carrying boxes. Inside one of the boxes, he spots a bright, almost golden powder. The last thing he sees before retreating is them handing a box to the two guards who move it further.  
- We decide to investigate before reporting anything and after a few shenanigans get up on the roof. Through a skylight, we see Khela distribute the powder. Some take it, some refuse. (Everyone wearing a cloak takes it) The people who swallow it enter a trance-like state for a while, and afterwards the congregation seems to end. The person who was unconscious on the stage previously is still laying there.  
- Khela talks to someone in the back, saying "we have to find out who those people (us) in the forest were and get the item back." Then she leaves, leaving the person on the stage and one guard behind.  
- We sneak down to investigate. Soren examines the unconscious person but cannot determine a cause. He is worried though as the person's eyes are open and the pupils different sizes. Laine finds some residue of the powder and scoops it up in a makeshift pouch.  
	- Aksel enters into the backroomm followed by Laine and Arkan and they obtain a pure portion of <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Gold Powder</span>.  
	- While Aksel looks through the room, Laine and Arkan go to the other room, where one person has been left and are joined by Soren who is worried about the unconscious person. Laine sneaks in and grapples the person who is inside, a smallfolk (dwarf) who is scribbling something at a desk.  
	- As Laine is grappling the guy and interrogating him, he is not willing to answer, so Soren uses Brain Drain on him.  
		- The group's name is the Unseeing Eye. The warehouse is their meeting spot. People come to celebrate the act of forgetting and the freedom of ignorance. It is a base of operations where "stuff" happens, though the person does not remember what the stuff is. The dwarf then dies of damage, after Soren learnt that this guy is just here for a job and to support his family. Laine takes the body and takes his stuff.  
	- Laine looks at the letter he was writing which basically conveys intent to his family to leave for a while for a job... which is in contradiction to what Soren learnt from the memories. The letter is signed with Ildoran, which is an elven name, so presumably not his own.  
	- We find more letters, about shipments to <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Strütze</span> and the hills signed by "T".  
	- Aksel finds notes by "T" and "K" claiming that "The hunter" found the item and they want to retrieve it and decide to have someone else clear the way and send two people afterwards to steal the item. Also notes proving that the cult collects, produces, and distributes something called <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Cytillesh</span>.  
- Suddenly guards knock, having been alerted to suspicious behavior but we manage to evade them and they go away. Eventually we decide that Aksel should get the unconscious person to a healer while the rest handles the corpse. We end up dumping them behind the warehouse with the gold powder to hopefully implicate the cult in this.  
- The party goes to sleep in their respective quarters.  
- Egil's workshop has finished the Baron's order and is planning to deliver it today. Aksel tells him of his findings, leaving out the murder, cautioning Sunny not to get involved.  
	- Egil mentions the order for the Baron was made for some party to meet with some "new religion". Aksel discovers the pieces look vaguely eye shaped.  
	- Egil then asks Aksel to get the party to deliver the pieces in his stead.  
- Laine gets woken up by Kany who tells them about a potentially lucrative heist during the Baron's party, as a kind of "initiation" into the Veiled Hand guild.  
- Soren researches the Cytillesh and finds out that it is a fungus that can be processed into a powder which makes the user forget everything happened within the last hour. The powder is golden. The fungi grow in damp caves so probably not in the north.  
	- Arkan remembers that Bordon had a similar drug and got it from someone new.
