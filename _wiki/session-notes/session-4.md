- Start as Audun is slowly waking up. However, he is disoriented and does not remember how he got here. We manage to convince him that we are friends and hand him back the journal we found. Curiously, he expected the journal to be close to him as he always keeps it with him, yet it was outside while he was inside the basement. He also does not remember the name of Uostas, which makes us think he may be afflicted with the Madness and recommend he gets himself checked out at the temple.  
- Since he also wants to head back to the city, we prepare to leave together. He remembers the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/egils-glasses/">glasses</a> and hands them to Aksel. However, as we exit the ruins, we notice that the sun is already setting and we have to camp here. We decide to camp in the basement for security.  
	- Soren examines the four statues. The four statues are representations of the four pillars. The one that was broken already was the statue of Tofrar.  
	- Arkan examines the books to gather 3 new words from the ancient language.  
	- Aksel just rests while Audun tries to do smalltalk.  
- On the next day, we continue our way back. Soren immediately gets the party lost and we encounter a bear but manage to chase it off. Audun takes over and leads us to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/lake-with-faces/">Lake with Faces</a> where we encounter <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Illeh</span>, a Nayad <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Trapped</span> who lives here as "Protector" of the lake. She says that her sister (The "Protector" of the forest) is not here and she is waiting, but is sad that no one comes to visit anymore. Soren learns through conversation that the last time she talked to people was probably during the time people woke up in Riku'To. Soren talks to her for a while and they discover a difference in opinion about the importance of knowledge. In the end, we have to leave and she sinks back into the lake. We decide to leave it be for now, as it may be too dangerous to tell people of her location, but Audun promises to visit her sometimes even if the party is not in the area.  
- Arkan and Audun talk about what they were looking for in the forest, and Audun does not seem to remember about Bordon or the Prata, though he makes a particular hand motion when pronouncing "Prata" by repeatedly putting is thumb and index and middlefinger together.  
- In the hunter camp we amaze the hunters with the story of how we punched the bear, then talk to Bordon who tells us about another person who was interested in the Prata while Soren gets high with him.  
- The way back to the city happens without incident, but Audun immediately gets grabbed by the guards at the city entrance and put back to work. The party splits off, with Aksel returning to <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/forgemaster-egil/">Egil</a>'s workshop. There he encounters Sunny crying to Egil because her boyfriend cheated on her with some elf girl. The party then reconvenes as they head towards the potion shop (name?).  
- Headpriestess of the Tofrar church is <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Shasenaya</span> a Leshny. She asks Soren to look into a new <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/purple-cloaks-with-one-eye/">cult</a> that is emerging that is supposedly meeting at the harbor.
