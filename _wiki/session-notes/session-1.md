- The campaign starts in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/uostas/">Uostas</a>, a port city close to <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/linna/">Linna</a>.  
- <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/aksel/">Aksel</a> awakes in his rented room, roused by the voice of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/forgemaster-egil/">Forgemaster Egil</a>(?), an old, small gnome. He asks if "the thing" is working fine which Aksel affirms, then sheepishly asks for a favor in return. He asks him to retrieve his glasses from a friend called <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/audan/">Audan</a> who lives across the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/saltwind-market/">Saltwind Market</a> and ask him if he is going to pay for the metalwork from last time. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/sunny/">Sunny</a>, Eagle's granddaughter comes with breakfast. Aksel has some food then leaves to look for the house.  
- <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/laine-beaufort/">Laine Beaufort</a> finds themselves in the bar of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/thieves-guild/">Thieves&#x27; Guild</a>. They were sent by a woman named <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/esise/">Esise</a>(?) to look for a half-orc named <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/kany/">Kany</a>. They are having breakfast and keep an eye out on the bar, before sitting down at a table with a group of elves. They propose a game of cards in exchange for information. After discovering that they are cheating, Laine asks them about Kany, who the elves seem to know. Kany usually comes in at 10 for her shift. Just as the game ends, Kany enters. As Laine approaches her, she seems ecstatic to hear that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/esise/">Esise</a> sent them and gives them the description of a person who has the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/necklace-of-hauling/">Necklace of Hauling</a> that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/esise/">Esise</a> is looking for. Laine then heads out to go to that person's house, passing <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/highrise/">Highrise</a>, the upper class district.  
- <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/soren-solvaren/">Soren Solvaren</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/arkan-raddil/">Arkan Raddil</a> randomly meet in front of the city gates, where there's a crowd gathering, trying to get into the city. They hear about <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/the-madness/">The Madness</a>, which everyone is apparently fleeing from, which manifests as sudden memory loss. Intrigued by this, they take notes and question people, until they get called to the front. They state their names and occupations and are allowed into the city, but notice that the scribe has not arrived to work yet. They offer to check up on them and receive a map.  
- All the party meet at the same house, but don't immediately take notice of each other. Aksel and Laine break in, while Soren and Arkan clock them as suspicious characters loitering around and get worried that the guy is being targeted by criminals. Aksel senses magic all over the house. Soren and Arkan sneak in behind them.  
- Aksel, Soren, and Arkan meet and after a short misunderstanding continue onwards. Laine hears that and sneaks away to the first floor. The trio finds a note and abandoned meal, and also discover the broken window (which had been there before and was increased by Laine.) Soren and Arkan discover a shelf with occult books and note down the titles and authors of interesting ones.  
- Meanwhile, Laine runs into clockwork hunters which ambush them and alert the others. They rush in and in the heat of battle team up against the constructs. After the battle they ally.  
- While Aksel and Laine keep looking for the items they have been asked to retrieve, Soren is looking over notes in the study, which describe a <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/prata/">Prata</a>, an item of an age long gone. He describes needing "the glasses" to identify the Prata, which maybe are the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/egils-glasses/">Glasses</a> that Aksel is looking for. Audan has been chasing down this item and a friend (<a class="wiki-link" href="/people/bordon/">Bordon</a>) has apparently located it, though the notes leave no hint at the location itselfs. Audan left a note saying

- Arkan looks over a bookshelf which has books about the previous age. Audan seems very interested in that time. Aksel and Laine enter a storeroom which is messy and blood on the floor and a crate. The crate is nailed shut. Laine finds a fancy ruby necklace. Arkan identifies it as magical and Soren identifies it as a Necklace of Hauling, which is the one that Laine was looking for.  
- Inside the crate they find an androgynous but probably male doll. As Laine closes the crate, the doll looks at them and grins. They also hear a noise from upstairs, despite there being no additional story, Aksel looks for the source and finds the trapdoor to the attic, where he finds a painting staring at him. It is, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/edvard/">Edvard</a> a "Living Painting" which can move on flat surfaces and seems to have some idea of what is going on. Together they go down into the study again where they bump into Laine on the way out.  
- The creature, seeing Laine carry items that don't belong to them, tries to snatch them back. Laine tricks it and keeps their items, including a <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/laines-crystal/">crystal</a> which seems to have some mental effect on people looking into it. Eventually the party learns from it that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/audan/">Audan</a> most likely is in the forests surrounding <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/uostas/">Uostas</a>. The party then leaves and parts ways, with Laine, Soren, and Arkan agreeing to meet later.  
- Laine returns to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/thieves-guild/">Thieves&#x27; Guild</a> and delivers the necklace, and offers to keep looking for <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/audan/">Audan</a>. Meanwhile Soren and Arkan return to the gate and give the information they learned. They learn that the forests are incredibly dangerous in the spring. They also learn that Bordon is a trapper, infamous for being a drunkard, and can most likely be met in some cheap bar in the city. He has also been arrested before. Arkan returns to meet Laine before, while Soren stays behind to help with the writing. Finally, Aksel returns without fulfilling his goal. He also offers to pursue Audan further.
