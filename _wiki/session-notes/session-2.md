- We start later in the evening on the same day. Laine is still in <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/thieves-guild/">The Bar</a> where they are joined by Soren and Arkan. They agree to look for Audan in the forest tomorrow. Soren and Arkan get a room and something to drink and eat. Soren goes gambling with the elves from earlier and wins 5 copper coins which he uses to buy a round of drinks for the table.  
- Aksel is tinkering on something with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/forgemaster-egil/">Egil</a> and talking about some new creation. Egil recommends going to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/hunters-camp/">Hunter&#x27;s Camp</a> before entering the forest proper to learn about the current conditions inside.  
- Everyone wakes up the next morning, doing their respective morning routines and having breakfast. We see lots of runnerboys calling out a missing person's notice describing <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/audan/">Audan</a> as a scribe, human, and having dark hair almost to his waist.  
- Aksel waits outside the gate and we meet up. On the way we talk about what brought us together, and Aksel mentioned seeing some lights on the mountains.  
- Eventually we arrive at the hunter's camp around noon and are greeted by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/aeth/">Aeth</a>, a huntress who invites us to lunch. As we ask her about Audun, she tells us that some people have seen him a few days ago, but has little confidence that he is still alive if he went deeper into the forest. To the south, the forest is still normal, but grows more thick and almost distorted towards the north. She mentions <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/pyrefowl/">Pyrefowl</a>s a type of bright bird with fire powers whose feathers fetch a pretty price. She also heard rumors of a "<a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/lake-with-faces/">Lake with Faces</a>" but expresses low confidence in them. She heard the rumor from <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/bordon/">Bordon</a>. There's also rumors about bandits. Basically don't be stupid.  
- <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/otar/">Otar</a>, an orcish man, was the one who spoke to Audun before, and mentioned that he was asking about the "weird part" in the north, and was talking about some ruins and an item he found.  
- We head deeper into the forest and immediately get lost. We run into some <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/shriekers/">Shriekers</a> and immediately afterwards into a bandit camp. While we manage to avoid a fight by agreeing to pay for protection money, Laine instead shows them the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/laines-crystal/">Crystal</a> which mesmerizes some of them and we jump them.  
- Laine finds a letter saying

- We bury the bodies and do short burial rites.  
- As we continue, we come across 2 people fleeing from the direction we were going. They are wearing <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/purple-cloaks-with-one-eye/">purple cloaks with one eye</a> embroidered on the back. We stop them and find that one is carrying a <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/wooden-box-with-arcane-runes/">wooden box with arcane runes</a> and the other has blood on their hands.
